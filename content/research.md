# My Research

I am currently working on various projects in the **AI** and **Data Science** fields. Below are some of the main areas of my research:

## 1. **AudioLLM - Audio-Based Large Language Models**

**What I am researching:**
- Integration of **audio processing** capabilities into existing **LLM architectures**.
- Efficient methods for achieving **cross-modality integration** between audio and text data.
- Benchmarks for evaluating the real-world performance of AudioLLMs.

**Current Outcomes:**
- **MERaLiON**: AudioLLM for Singlish and other related tasks.
- **AudioBench**: Evaluation toolkit for AudioLLMs.

## 2. **Multilingual and Multicultural LLMs**

**What I am researching:**
- Properties that multilingual LLMs should possess to cater to **low-resource languages**.
- Approaches for **efficient multilingual learning**.
- Mechanisms for **robust multilingual knowledge alignment** within models.

**Current Outcomes:**
- **SeaEval**: Multilingual evaluation for LLMs.
- **CRAFT**: Cross-lingual transfer learning models.
