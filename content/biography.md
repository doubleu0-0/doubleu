# About Me

I am a **Data Science** enthusiast with a keen interest in **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Machine Learning (ML)**. I am currently in my 3rd year of studies at **Ngee Ann Polytechnic** in Singapore.

Before my studies, I obtained my **bachelor's degree** from **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)** in 2017.

In 2021, I joined the **National University of Singapore (NUS)** as a research fellow working with **Prof. Haizhou Li**, where I focused on AI and ML research. 

Currently, I am conducting research in **AI for Data Science**, exploring areas such as **Audio-Based Large Language Models** and **Multilingual LLMs**.
